Home Appliances Kharidne Se Pehle Yeh Checklist Padho

TV, refrigerator, washing machine, AC aur kitchen appliances ko samajhkar compare karne ke liye simple guide.

Important: Sirf discount dekhkar product mat chuno. Exact model number, current price, installation charges, warranty aur tumhare area mein service availability zaroor check karo.

1. TV: Screen size aur picture quality

TV kharidne se pehle room mein viewing distance aur wall/cabinet ki jagah measure karo. 4K resolution ke saath brightness, panel type, HDR support, refresh rate aur HDMI ports bhi compare karo. Gaming ke liye console compatibility aur input lag check karna useful hai.

2. Refrigerator: Capacity aur electricity use

Family size aur daily storage ke hisaab se litres choose karo. Kitchen ki width, height aur door khulne ki jagah measure karo. Inverter compressor, convertible modes aur current BEE energy label compare karo. Energy rating aur annual electricity consumption ko exact model ke label se verify karo.

3. Washing machine: Load, space aur wash programs

Capacity ko laundry quantity ke hisaab se choose karo. Front-load aur top-load models ka water use, wash cycles, noise, dimensions aur installation requirements compare karo. Sirf “inverter” ya “steam” jaise feature names dekhkar decision mat lo; exact model mein feature included hai ya nahi, check karo.

4. Air conditioner: Room ke hisaab se capacity

AC ki capacity room size, sunlight, ceiling height aur local climate par depend karti hai. 1-ton ya 1.5-ton ko bina room assess kiye blindly select mat karo. Current BEE star label/ISEER, installation charges, copper pipe length, drain setup aur warranty terms confirm karo. Installation qualified technician se karwao.

5. Kitchen appliances: Actual use pe focus

Microwave, air fryer, mixer ya dishwasher ke liye capacity, dimensions, power requirement, cleaning, included accessories aur replacement parts ki cost compare karo. Aise features ke liye extra paisa mat do jo tum use hi nahi karoge.

6. Water aur air purifiers

Water purifier select karte waqt water source aur water-quality report samjho; har ghar ko same purification system ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Filter replacement, service cost aur water wastage check karo. Air purifier ke liye room size ke against CADR, replacement filter price aur noise level compare karo.

7. Total cost calculate karo

  • Product ki current price aur delivery fee.
  • Installation, accessories, stabilizer ya extra parts ki cost.
  • Bijli/paani ka expected use aur replacement filters/consumables.
  • Warranty duration, service centre aur return/replacement rules.

8. Purchase se pehle final verification

Brand ki official website par exact model code match karo. Retailer listing par seller name, variant, included accessories, invoice, warranty aur return window check karo. Similar naam wale models ki specifications alag ho sakti hain. Price aur stock change ho sakte hain, isliye checkout se pehle dobara verify karo.

Nivoget ka approach: Yeh guide general buying information ke liye hai; hum is page par hands-on testing ka claim nahi karte. Final decision se pehle official specifications aur seller terms check karo.
