Diwali Shopping: Electronics Kharidne Se Pehle Yeh Checklist Padho

Phone, laptop, TV aur home appliances ko smartly compare karne ke liye simple India-focused guide.

Sabse important rule: Sirf discount percentage mat dekho. Final payable price aur exact model number compare karo. Price, stock, bank offers aur return policy kabhi bhi change ho sakte hain.

1. Sale se pehle shortlist banao

Apni need, maximum budget aur compare karne ke liye teen models likh lo. Exact model code aur specifications manufacturer ki official website par check karo. Same naam jaise dikhne wale products mein RAM, storage, screen size, energy rating ya accessories alag ho sakte hain.

2. Banner nahi, final price compare karo

Bank offer, exchange value, coupon, delivery, installation aur zaroori accessories jodne ke baad actual payable amount nikalo. Check karo discount kis price ke comparison mein dikhaya ja raha hai. Countdown timer ya “limited stock” dekhkar jaldi mein purchase mat karo.

3. Seller, warranty aur return policy check karo

Seller ka naam, invoice, manufacturer warranty, return/replacement window aur open-box delivery rules padho. Confirm karo warranty India mein valid hai aur installation authorised service partner se karwana zaroori hai ya nahi. Invoice aur order confirmation save karke rakho.

4. Phone aur laptop checklist

  • Phone: RAM/storage variant, battery, charging support, software updates, service network aur zaroorat ke hisaab se 5G band compatibility check karo.
  • Laptop: processor apne kaam ke hisaab se choose karo; RAM upgrade, SSD, screen, battery, ports, operating system aur warranty check karo.
  • Accessories: compatibility, connector, power rating aur genuine warranty verify karo. Sasta accessory kaam ka nahi agar woh tumhare device ke saath compatible hi nahi.

5. TV aur home appliances checklist

  • TV: viewing distance aur furniture ki jagah measure karo; panel, brightness, refresh rate, HDMI ports aur apps compare karo.
  • Fridge: installation space aur doorway measure karo; capacity, current energy label par annual consumption aur local service check karo.
  • Washing machine: capacity, type, dimensions, water use, wash programs, noise aur installation requirements compare karo.
  • AC: room size aur heat load ke hisaab se capacity choose karo; current energy label, installation cost aur service terms verify karo.
  • Kitchen appliances: usable capacity, power rating, safety features, cleaning aur spare parts availability check karo.

6. Total ownership cost samjho

Appliance ke purchase price ke saath electricity, installation, accessories, filters/consumables aur maintenance ko bhi dhyan mein rakho. Kabhi thoda mehenga model tumhari need ke liye better ho sakta hai—but sirf feature name dekhkar assume mat karo ki paise bachenge.

7. Proof save karo aur delivery inspect karo

Checkout par model, seller, price aur offer terms ke screenshots save karo. Delivery par package, model number aur visible condition check karo; seller ke delivery process ko follow karo. Damage ya mismatch ho toh jaldi report karo aur santusht hone tak packaging sambhal kar rakho.

Payment se pehle quick checklist

  • Exact model number aur variant confirm
  • Final price aur offer ki eligibility samajh li
  • Seller, invoice, warranty aur return terms check
  • Delivery aur installation charges include
  • Official specs aur local service check
  • Purchase planned budget mein hai—sirf sale deadline ki wajah se nahi
