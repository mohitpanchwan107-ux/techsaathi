Editorial Policy

Our goal is to explain technology in clear language and help readers identify what matters for their needs and budget.

Research and accuracy

Guides should use product documentation and reputable sources. Specifications, software support, warranty terms and prices can change; confirm current details with manufacturers and sellers.

Testing claims

Nivoget will not describe a product as personally tested unless hands-on testing actually took place and the page explains the method. Research should not be presented as original testing.

Corrections and updates

Content should be reviewed when important details change. Readers should use the contact method published on the site when one is available.

Independence

Recommendations should explain use cases and trade-offs, not claim one product is best for everyone. Sponsorship or affiliate relationships should be disclosed clearly.
