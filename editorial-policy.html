Editorial Policy

Nivoget aims to make technology information understandable and useful for readers comparing products and learning about features.

Research and accuracy

Product information should be checked against manufacturer documentation and reputable sources. Model names, specifications, software support, prices, availability and warranty terms may change by region and configuration. Readers should verify details before purchasing.

Product images and catalogue status

Some catalogue cards use illustrative category previews rather than verified photographs of the exact model. A card that links to a manufacturer catalogue may describe a product family rather than one specific SKU. These distinctions should remain clear to readers.

Testing claims

Nivoget does not describe a product as personally tested unless hands-on testing actually took place and the page explains the method. Research and manufacturer claims are not the same as independent testing.

Corrections and updates

We aim to correct errors when identified. Readers can report an issue through the public GitHub feedback link on the Contact page. Do not include private or sensitive information in public reports.

Independence and disclosures

Recommendations should explain use cases and trade-offs rather than claim one product is best for everyone. Any future sponsorship or affiliate relationship should be disclosed clearly.
