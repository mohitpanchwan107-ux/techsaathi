Diwali Electronics Shopping Checklist

A practical India-focused guide for choosing phones, laptops, TVs and home appliances without getting distracted by a big discount label.

Core rule: compare the final payable price and exact model number—not just the percentage discount. Prices, stock, bank offers and return policies can change.

1. Make a shortlist before the sale

Write down what you need, your maximum budget and three models worth comparing. Check the official manufacturer page for the exact model code and key specifications. Similar-looking products may have different RAM, storage, screen sizes, energy ratings or included accessories.

2. Compare the final price, not the banner

Calculate what you will actually pay after eligible bank offers, exchange value, coupons, delivery, installation and required accessories. Check what price the discount is based on. Do not buy only because a countdown timer or “limited stock” message creates urgency.

3. Check seller, warranty and returns

Read the seller name, invoice details, manufacturer warranty, return or replacement window and open-box delivery rules. Confirm the warranty is valid in India and whether installation must be done by an authorised service partner. Keep the invoice and order confirmation.

4. Phone and laptop checklist

  • Phone: verify storage/RAM variant, battery and charging support, software-update policy, service network and relevant 5G band compatibility.
  • Laptop: match the processor to your workload; check RAM upgradeability, SSD, display, battery, ports, operating system and warranty.
  • Accessories: confirm compatibility, connector type, power rating and genuine warranty.

5. TV and home-appliance checklist

  • TV: measure viewing distance and furniture space; compare panel, brightness, refresh rate, HDMI ports and apps.
  • Refrigerator: measure installation space and doorways; compare capacity, current energy-label consumption and local service.
  • Washing machine: compare capacity, type, dimensions, water use, wash programs, noise and installation.
  • Air conditioner: choose capacity for room size and heat load; verify the current energy label, installation cost and service terms.
  • Kitchen appliances: check usable capacity, power rating, safety features, cleaning needs and replacement-part availability.

6. Estimate total ownership cost

For appliances, include electricity use, installation, accessories, filters or consumables and maintenance. A higher purchase price may be reasonable if a product fits your needs better, but do not assume a feature automatically saves money.

7. Keep proof and inspect delivery

Save screenshots of the model, seller, price and offer terms at checkout. On delivery, check the package, model number and visible condition while following the seller’s process. Report damage or a mismatch promptly and keep packaging until satisfied.

Quick pre-payment checklist

  • Exact model number and variant confirmed
  • Final price and offer eligibility understood
  • Seller, invoice, warranty and return terms checked
  • Delivery and installation charges included
  • Official specifications and local service checked
  • Purchase fits the planned budget—not just the sale deadline

Editorial note: Nivoget provides buying guidance, not live price tracking or a guarantee that a deal is the cheapest. Verify current terms on manufacturer and seller pages before paying.
