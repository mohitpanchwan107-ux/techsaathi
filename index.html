SMARTPHONES · BUYING GUIDE

How to choose a smartphone

Know what matters before choosing your next phone.

Read guide

Performance: Compare the exact processor and independent performance reviews.

Battery: Look for real-world battery tests and supported charging speeds.

Camera: Compare sample photographs in daylight and low light.

Updates: Check how long the manufacturer promises software and security updates.

Value: Include warranty, service availability and storage in your decision.

LAPTOPS · BUYING GUIDE

What laptop specifications matter?

Choose a laptop that suits your study or work.

Read guide

RAM: Consider how many applications you need to run together.

Storage: An SSD usually makes everyday use more responsive.

Processor: Compare the exact generation and real performance reviews.

Display: Check resolution, brightness and screen size.

Compatibility: Verify operating-system support for the software you use.
