SMARTPHONES · 6 MIN READ

How to choose a smartphone

Understand performance, battery, display, camera and software support before choosing a phone.

Read guide

Performance: Check real-world reviews and the processor generation, not just marketing claims.

Battery: Look for independent battery tests and charging support.

Camera: Compare sample photos in daylight and low light.

Updates: Verify the manufacturer's software-support policy for the exact model.

Budget: Compare total value, warranty and service availability before buying.

💻
LAPTOPS · 5 MIN READ

What laptop specifications matter?

Choose sensible specifications for school, office work, programming or creative tasks.

Read guide

RAM: 8 GB is a useful starting point for basic modern multitasking; demanding work may need more.

Storage: An SSD usually improves everyday responsiveness compared with an HDD.

Processor: Compare the exact generation and independent performance tests.

Display: Check resolution, brightness and size in person when possible.

Compatibility: Confirm operating-system support for the apps you need.

How to compare products properly

A simple checklist to help avoid paying extra for features you will never use.

Read guide

Compare exact model numbers rather than product family names.

Check warranty, return rules, seller reputation and service availability.

Read multiple independent reviews and verify the final checkout price.
